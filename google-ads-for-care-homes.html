Google Ads for Care Homes

Help families find residential personal care that fits their needs. Build search campaigns around the home, the support it offers and a clear route to a conversation or visit.

Talk to AlexRead the strategy

Campaign focus

A room enquiry is not yet a suitable placement.

Match the requested support to the home’s actual provision before treating an enquiry as a marketing success.

This guide is for operators marketing accommodation with personal care. It is not a generic campaign plan for every senior-living setting. Care home can include nursing provision, especially in UK usage; nursing services need their own campaign and assessment route. Assisted living and retirement housing should not be used as replacement labels for residential care.

For campaigns promoting nursing provision rather than residential personal care, use the nursing home and skilled nursing marketing hub. A mixed-service operator may need both approaches, on clearly separated service pages.

Define the Residential Offer Before Choosing Keywords

Write down which home, room types and personal-care services the campaign promotes. Identify whether respite stays or dementia support are available and how suitability is assessed. A family asking for short-term respite should not receive an ad that only describes permanent accommodation.

Agree who updates availability and who responds to enquiries. Advertise a visit or a conversation when that is the real next step, rather than implying that a room can be booked online.

Build Keyword Groups Around the Family’s Decision

Separate the home’s name, local residential-care searches and supported care options. The examples below are planning ideas, not search-volume or performance claims.

Do not add “assisted living,” “nursing facility” or “retirement apartments” simply to increase reach. Only use them when the advertised service genuinely matches that search.

Exclude Wrong-Service Searches Without Losing Families

Review actual queries for recruitment, training, supplies and home-care visits when those are not the offer. If the home does not provide nursing care, investigate nursing-service queries before expanding the campaign. Do not automatically exclude “cost” or “funding”: families often need those answers before arranging a visit.

Plan for Relatives Who Live Outside the Catchment

The home is local, but the person researching it may live elsewhere. Start with a controlled geographic plan and review the intended home location during enquiry handling. If remote relatives produce suitable enquiries, test that demand separately instead of expanding every campaign to a whole country.

Make the Landing Page Feel Like the Actual Home

Show current rooms, shared areas, meals, activities and the people a family will meet. Explain the support provided, what is not available, how fees are discussed and what a first visit involves. Separate permanent and respite enquiries if the process differs.

Include current regulator or inspection information where relevant, with its date and source. Avoid presenting an old rating, a room photograph or a staged scene as proof of current availability.

Test Clear Ads Rather Than Emotional Pressure

An illustrative message is “Residential Care in [Town] — Explore Rooms and Arrange a Visit.” Use it only if the service and visit offer are accurate. A respite ad should explain the temporary-stay enquiry path instead. Do not imply that the advertiser knows a viewer is struggling to care for a parent.

Keep Enquiry Forms Short and Assessment Separate

Ask for a contact method, preferred home, broad service interest and timing. A public advertising form is not a care assessment. Explain who will call back, then arrange any sensitive discussion through the home’s approved process.

Record whether the family was reached, whether the requested support fits, and whether a visit or assessment was arranged. A low form cost means little if the home cannot help the people responding.

Budget Against Visits, Suitability and Move-ins

Track enquiry → conversation → visit → assessment → move-in, adapting the order to the home’s process. Review losses such as unsupported care, location, timing, unavailable rooms or no response. Keep permanent and respite results separate because their economics differ.

Use your own qualified-enquiry and move-in rates to set a test budget. Check room capacity and staff response capacity before increasing spend. Neither a universal lead price nor a fixed timeline can tell you whether the campaign will work for this home.

A 90-Day Residential-Care Search Plan

Days 1–30

Map the residential and respite offers, gather home-specific proof, build the enquiry page and agree who handles visits and assessments.

Days 31–60

Test focused local search groups. Review wrong-service queries, family contact rates and reasons visits do not happen.

Days 61–90

Compare suitable enquiries and completed visits by campaign. Improve the weakest step before expanding reach or budget.

Check Service Definitions and Current Platform Rules

The NHS explanation of residential and nursing homes shows why care home is not a synonym for one care level. Check Google’s restricted-targeting policy for the actual advertised service and market. These marketing examples do not determine care eligibility or legal compliance.

Google Ads for Care Homes FAQ

Should a residential care home bid on nursing-home keywords?

Only where the advertised home actually provides the nursing service sought. Otherwise, those clicks can create unsuitable enquiries and confuse families.

Can the campaign target relatives outside the local area?

It can be tested where the service location is clear and enquiries show a genuine fit. Keep that test separate so you can judge its quality.

What is a useful first conversion?

A reachable enquiry about support the home provides is more useful than a form alone. Track visits, suitability assessments and move-ins afterward.

Should care fees appear on the landing page?

Explain the actual fee-discussion process and what information affects a quote. Publish prices only when they are accurate, current and clear about what is included.
