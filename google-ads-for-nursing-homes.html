Google Ads for Nursing Homes

Capture active local demand with focused keywords, accurate service pages, responsible messaging, admissions tracking, and budgets tied to qualified outcomes.

The central rule

Do not treat every enquiry as an admission.

Campaigns should connect searches to suitable services and measure the complete path from click to qualified admissions outcome.

This cluster uses US terminology. Nursing homes and skilled nursing facilities provide a different level of care from non-clinical assisted living and retirement communities. This guide focuses on the search-to-enquiry path for nursing homes, skilled nursing facilities and SNFs.

Written and reviewed by Alex · Updated October 2026

Start With Services, Locations and Availability

Document the services the organization wants to grow, the locations families can realistically consider, current capacity, admissions requirements, response hours, and commercial priorities. A broad campaign cannot fix unclear availability or a slow handoff.

Structure Campaigns Around Search Intent

Separate brand searches, general local searches, service-specific searches, and location campaigns. Keep distinct care levels in separate ad groups or campaigns so the ad and landing page answer the same need. Use call-focused paths only when trained staff can respond.

Build a Deliberate Negative Keyword List

Review search terms before excluding queries. Common starting points may include jobs, careers, CNA training, salary, school, definition, free, equipment, but a word should be excluded only when it is genuinely irrelevant. Protect useful informational searches when the website has a helpful path from research to enquiry.

Control Geography Carefully

Target the actual catchment area, use presence-based location settings where appropriate, and review where enquiries originate. Families may search from outside the area on behalf of someone local, so interpret geography with the full decision journey rather than a rigid radius alone.

Write Accurate, Respectful Ads

Use clear service and location language, a realistic next step, and verifiable proof. Avoid fear-based copy, unsupported rankings, guaranteed outcomes, or wording that implies Google knows a person's medical condition. Review current Google Ads personalized advertising, healthcare, housing, privacy, and local regulatory requirements.

Send Each Ad to the Closest Useful Page

The landing page should explain the service, who it may suit, the location, environment, staff or credentials, admissions process, costs or payment context where appropriate, availability language, and next step. Show facility credentials, service definitions, staff expertise, therapy information, real environment photography, admissions guidance, visiting details, and accurate third-party information families can verify.

Track Calls and Forms Beyond the Platform

Capture required level of care, referral source, location, timing, payer or insurance context where appropriate, current setting, clinical documents needed for review, and the preferred admissions contact route. Connect the enquiry source to qualified admissions calls, referral submissions, document reviews, tours, accepted admissions, completed admissions, payer mix where appropriate, and lost-admission reasons. Use call recording or transcription only when lawful, disclosed, secured, and appropriate for the organization.

Set a Budget From the Admissions Model

Estimate search demand, expected click cost, landing-page conversion, qualification, progression, admission rate, capacity, and the value of an appropriate admission. There is no universal cost-per-lead benchmark that replaces the organization's own economics and service obligations.

Optimize in the Right Order

  1. Confirm conversion tracking and admissions outcomes.
  2. Remove irrelevant searches and unsupported locations.
  3. Improve the ad-to-page match.
  4. Compare services, locations, schedules, and devices.
  5. Adjust bids and budgets using qualified outcome data.

A Practical 90-Day Google Ads Plan

Month 1

Define services, keyword groups, negatives, locations, pages, policy review, and tracking.

Month 2

Launch narrowly, review search terms and calls, and repair the conversion path.

Month 3

Shift budget using qualified enquiries, tours or reviews, and admissions outcomes.

Frequently asked questions

Google Ads for Nursing Homes FAQ

Can Google Ads work for nursing homes?

Yes, when the paid-search campaign reflects real services, geography, availability, policy requirements, a trustworthy conversion path, and an admissions team that records outcomes.

What should count as a conversion?

Start with meaningful steps such as qualified admissions calls, referral submissions, document reviews, tours, accepted admissions, completed admissions, payer mix where appropriate, and lost-admission reasons. Use one set of definitions across advertising, analytics, and admissions.

How quickly should enquiries receive a response?

Respond as quickly and consistently as the organization can sustain. Confirm receipt immediately, explain the next step, and route urgent or professional referrals appropriately.

What claims should ads avoid?

Avoid unsupported superlatives, guaranteed outcomes, inaccurate availability, and wording that implies knowledge of a person’s health or other sensitive attributes. Review current platform and local rules before launch.
